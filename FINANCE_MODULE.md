# Finance Module

## Implemented
- `/finance` dashboard shows revenue, bookings, refunds, wallet, coupons, payments, failures, and route revenue from live collections.
- `/payments`, `/payments/history`, and `/payments/[id]` show payment records.
- `/reports` exposes summary report data and CSV-compatible exports.
- `/audit` exposes audit records and CSV export when requested.

## Remaining
- Binary Excel/PDF generation, GST invoice templates, payment settlement reconciliation, and charts beyond KPI cards.
