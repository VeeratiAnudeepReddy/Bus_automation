# Dispatch Center

## Screen
`/dispatcher`

## Capabilities
- Today's trips
- Active/upcoming/delayed trips
- Driver and conductor availability
- Incidents
- Leave queue
- Links to trips, schedules, incidents, leave, buses, drivers, conductors, and calendar

## APIs
- `GET /api/dispatcher/dashboard`
- `POST /api/trips/:id/actions`
- `POST /api/trips/:id/location`
