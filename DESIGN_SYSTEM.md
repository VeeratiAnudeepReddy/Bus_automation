# Design System

## Layout
All restored modules use:
- Enterprise shell.
- Hero card.
- KPI cards.
- Chart row.
- Map card where location context applies.
- Searchable/filterable table.
- Activity queue.
- Details drawer panel.

## Visual Language
- White cards on zinc workspace background.
- Dark hero sections for primary context.
- Rounded `2xl` and `3xl` containers.
- Colored status badges.
- Soft borders and hover states.

## Status Colors
- Green: completed, active, captured, processed.
- Blue: running, authorized, published.
- Yellow: pending, draft, created.
- Orange: maintenance, processing, overdue.
- Red: failed, rejected, cancelled.
- Gray: inactive or neutral states.
