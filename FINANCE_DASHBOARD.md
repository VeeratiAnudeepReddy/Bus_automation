# Finance Dashboard

## Endpoint
`GET /api/finance/dashboard`

## Includes
- Ticket revenue.
- Daily, monthly, and yearly revenue placeholders.
- Refund totals.
- Wallet balance.
- Coupon usage.
- Pending and failed payments.
- Ledger revenue, gateway revenue, wallet revenue, refunds, tax, and coupons.
- Recent payments.
- Recent ledger entries.

## Access
Finance and operations roles can view reports through existing RBAC.
