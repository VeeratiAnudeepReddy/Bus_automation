# Posts Module

Posts are organization-scoped announcements with author, visibility, roles, pinned state, priority, category, tags, attachments, read receipts, publish/expiry dates, comments, likes, soft delete, and edit history.

Routes:
- `GET /api/posts`
- `GET /api/posts/:id`
- `POST /api/posts`
- `PATCH /api/posts/:id`
- `DELETE /api/posts/:id`
- comment, like, and pin routes

Frontend:
- `/posts`
- `/posts/new`
- `/posts/[id]`
